We camped at
[Millersylvania State Park](https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/millersylvania-state-park)
for the July 4th weekend, an annual family event. Given it was going to be a good
few days, a good location, and we had a nice sized site, I brought a whole
selection of toys to play with.

## Antenna

The Chameleon "Mil" Whip
([CHA MIL WHIP](https://chameleonantenna.com/collections/vertical-antennas/products/cha-mil-whip-2-0))
and extension
([CHA MIL EXT](https://chameleonantenna.com/collections/vertical-antennas/products/cha-mil-ext-2-0))
on a Bosch
[BT160](https://www.boschtools.com/us/en/products/bt160-0601091210) contractors
tripod using the Chameleon
([CHA SURVEYOR-A](https://chameleonantenna.com/products/cha-surveyor-a-surveyor-tripod-adapter))
adapter.

![Chameleon Mil Vertical](/assets/img/posts/ham-camping-millersylvania-champv.jpeg)

The Chameleon tactical delta loop
([CHA TDL](https://chameleonantenna.com/products/tactical-delta-loop-tdl))
mounted on the Chameleon
[carbon fiber tripod](https://chameleonantenna.com/collections/mounting-systems/products/carbon-fiber-tripod)
with two 27" extensions
([CHA SINGLE-EXT](https://chameleonantenna.com/products/cha-single-ext-pack-of-2));
which sounds crazy but as it was a wind-free weekend it seemed pretty stable.

![Chameleon Tactical Delta Loop](/assets/img/posts/ham-camping-millersylvania-chatdl.jpeg)

That's a lot of Chameleon products, and believe me, I have **a  lot** more --
here is one of their [sling bags](https://chameleonantenna.com/collections/field-support-equipment/products/cha-sling-bag)
I use to carry some of the collection out with me.

![Chameleon Collection](/assets/img/posts/ham-camping-millersylvania-chameleon-collection.jpeg)

The final antenna system up was the fantastic Buddipole!

![Buddipole Deluxe](/assets/img/posts/ham-camping-millersylvania-buddipole.jpeg)

## Radios

I took two rigs with me, the Lab599
[TX-500](https://lab599.com/products/tproduct/506522908-147130382471-tx-500-discovery)
and the Elecraft
[KX3](https://elecraft.com/products/kx3-all-mode-160-6-m-transceiver). I also
keep a venerable Yaesu
[VX-6](https://www.yaesu.com/product-detail.aspx?Model=VX-6R&CatName=VHF/UHF%20Handhelds)
in the car at all times so I also had 2m/70cm coverage. As for cables I used a
a 50' reel of Messi and Paoloni
[PotaFlex](https://www.gigaparts.com/messi-and-paoloni-potaflex-7-coax-cable.html)
and Chameleon cables.

![Radio station](/assets/img/posts/ham-camping-millersylvania-station.jpeg)

In the picture you can also see my MacBook Pro, Elecraft bag, Nestout gear
case for the TX-500. You can also see the POTAFlex cable in all it's bright
colored glory on the right. The small fold-out stand/rack is from Ali Express,
cheap simple but effective.
